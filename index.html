How it works

Private by design,
because nothing ever leaves.

Privlio has no servers and no accounts. Every contact you add lives on your device, and the sensitive ones go behind a PIN.

1
Add contacts — they stay on your device
Capture a name, role, company, email, phone, tags, and a note. Everything is written straight to local storage on your phone. There's no account to create and no server to send it to.
2
Move sensitive people into the vault
Tap "Add to Vault" on any contact and they disappear from your main list into a private, PIN-protected space — perfect for investors, legal contacts, and personal relationships you'd rather keep off-screen.
3
Lock the vault with a 4-digit PIN
Your vault contacts are encrypted and unlock only with your PIN. Nobody who picks up your phone can browse your most private relationships — and there are no servers anywhere that hold them.
4
Back up and move on your terms
Export your entire network to a JSON file whenever you want, and import it back on a new device. You're never locked in, and your data never passes through us.

Because Privlio stores everything on-device, the way to protect against losing your phone is to export a backup. From Settings → Export contacts, save your network as a JSON file somewhere safe. On a new device, use Import contacts to restore it. If you lose your only device without a backup, your data can't be recovered — that's the trade-off for keeping it entirely off the cloud.
Privlio is built by a Canadian company and respects Canadian law. But there is nothing for us to disclose: we don't run servers, we don't have accounts, and we never receive your contacts.
