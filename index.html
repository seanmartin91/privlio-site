How it works

Private by design,
encrypted before it leaves.

Your contacts are encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know, then synced so you can reach them anywhere. We only ever store ciphertext.

1
Add contacts on any device
Capture a name, role, company, email, phone, tags, and a note. Add on your phone and see it on the web — your network follows you across devices.
2
Encrypted with your passphrase
Before anything syncs, every contact is end-to-end encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know. Your passphrase never reaches our servers.
3
We store only ciphertext
All our servers ever hold is an encrypted blob — mathematical noise without your passphrase. We can't read your contacts, and neither can anyone who breaches, subpoenas, or buys the database.
4
Lock your most private people away
Move sensitive contacts into a PIN-protected vault, hidden from your main list. And you can export your whole network to a JSON file anytime — you're never locked in.
